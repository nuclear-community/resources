# 📰 Reading & news

Journals, news sources, landmark reports, podcasts, and blogs — how to stay current without drowning.

## Journals

| Journal | Publisher | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Nuclear Science and Engineering* | ANS | The field's foundational journal — reactor physics, transport, nuclear data. |
| *Nuclear Technology* | ANS | Applied: fuels, thermal-hydraulics, safety, SMRs. |
| *Annals of Nuclear Energy* | Elsevier | Broad reactor-physics and energy research. |
| *Nuclear Engineering and Design* | Elsevier | Plant and component design, safety analysis. |
| *Progress in Nuclear Energy* | Elsevier | Systems, fuel cycle, policy-adjacent engineering. |
| *Journal of Nuclear Materials* | Elsevier | Fuels and materials science. |
| *Fusion Science and Technology* | ANS | The fusion side. |
| *Health Physics* | Wolters Kluwer | Radiation protection. |

> Access tip: many are paywalled. Check [INIS](https://inis.iaea.org/) (free bibliographic database with full texts) and institutional access; ANS journals have open-access options, and a large share of relevant work also appears as free IAEA/NEA/NRC reports.

## News

| Source | Cadence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [World Nuclear News](https://world-nuclear-news.org/) | daily | The industry's wire service — factual, global, fast. |
| [ANS Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news) | daily/weekly | US-centric with good technical explainers. |
| [NEI](https://nei.org/) (US industry institute) | weekly+ | Policy and industry positions; read as the industry voice. |
| [IAEA News](https://www.iaea.org/newscenter) | weekly | The international agency's perspective. |
| [OECD NEA News](https://www.oecd-nea.org/) | periodic | Research and policy briefs. |

## Landmark free reports (the canon)

| Report | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| IAEA SSR-2/1, *Safety of Nuclear Power Plants: Design* | The global design-safety baseline. |
| IAEA, *The Fukushima Daiichi Accident* (2015 report) | The definitive technical account. |
| INSAG-7, *The Chernobyl Accident* | The IAEA's sober reassessment — safety culture as a concept was born here. |
| NUREG-1150, *Severe Accident Risks* (NRC) | The landmark probabilistic risk study of US plants. |
| MIT, *The Future of Nuclear Energy in a Carbon-Constrained World* (2018) | The reference on nuclear & climate strategy. |
| IPCC AR6 / SR1.5 energy chapters | Nuclear's lifecycle emissions and decarbonization role, as assessed by the IPCC. |
| NEA/IAEA *Uranium* ("Red Book") | Resources and demand, updated every two years. |

## Reading strategy

- **Daily:** one news source is enough (WNN). Skim, don't binge.
- **Weekly:** one technical thing — a journal abstract, an NRC/IAEA report chapter.
- **Monthly:** one long read — a landmark report section or a podcast while commuting.
- **Always:** ask "what's the primary source?" before repeating any number.
