# 🗃️ Data & databases

Numbers you can look up — nuclides, reactors, documents, energy statistics.

## Nuclide & nuclear data

| Database | Access | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NuDat 3 (NNDC)](https://www.nndc.bnl.gov/nudat3/) | free | The chart of nuclides, interactive: decay modes, half-lives, cross sections. The tab-keeper of every nuclear physicist. |
| [IAEA LiveChart of Nuclides](https://www-nds.iaea.org/relnsd/vcharthtml/VChartHTML.html) | free | IAEA's answer to NuDat; clean UI and decay searches. |
| [IAEA Nuclear Data Services](https://www-nds.iaea.org/) | free | Point of entry for ENDF/decay/thermal scattering data. |
| [NNDC (Brookhaven)](https://www.nndc.bnl.gov/) | free | ENSDF, EXFOR (experimental reaction data), CINDA, and more. |
| [OECD NEA Data Bank](https://www.oecd-nea.org/tools/db/) | free (some registration) | JEFF library, computer program services, benchmarks (IRPhEP, ICSBEP). |

## Reactors & operations

| Database | Access | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IAEA PRIS](https://pris.iaea.org/pris/) | free | Every power reactor in the world: status, capacity factors, construction history. The reference for "how many reactors…" questions. |
| [NRC ADAMS](https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/adams.html) | free | The full public document archive of the US regulator — licensing documents, inspection reports, event reports. |
| NEA/IAEA *Uranium* ("Red Book") | free summary / paid full | Periodic assessment of global uranium resources, production, and demand. |

## Literature & documents

| Database | Access | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IAEA INIS](https://inis.iaea.org/) | free | International Nuclear Information System — the field's bibliographic database, decades deep, with many full texts. |
| [IAEA publications](https://www.iaea.org/publications) | free | Safety standards, Nuclear Energy Series, technical reports — thousands of free PDFs. |
| [NRC NUREG series](https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/nuregs/) | free | Landmark US technical reports: NUREG-1150 (PSA), NUREG/CR-series research. |
| [OECD NEA publications](https://www.oecd-nea.org/jcms/pl_39910/publications) | mostly free | CSNI reports, safety research, fuel-cycle economics. |

## Energy & open data

| Database | Access | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Our World in Data — Energy](https://ourworldindata.org/energy) | free (open data) | Capacity factors, deaths per TWh, CO₂ per kWh, country profiles — all downloadable. The source to cite in energy debates. |
| [Ember — electricity data](https://ember-climate.org/data/) | free | Hourly/monthly generation data by country — see nuclear's real grid role. |
