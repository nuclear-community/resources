# 🏛️ Organizations

Who's who in the nuclear world — societies to join, bodies to know, regulators to cite.

## International bodies

| Organization | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [IAEA](https://www.iaea.org/) | The UN's nuclear agency: safety standards, safeguards, technical cooperation, PRIS, INIS. Its [publications](https://www.iaea.org/publications) are a free library in themselves. |
| [OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (NEA)](https://www.oecd-nea.org/) | Inter-governmental nuclear cooperation: safety research, data bank, fuel-cycle economics, benchmarks. |
| [World Nuclear Association (WNA)](https://www.world-nuclear.org/) | Industry association; its [information library](https://www.world-nuclear.org/information-library) is the most accessible country-by-country and technology reference. |
| [ICRP](https://www.icrp.org/) | The International Commission on Radiological Protection — where dose limits and protection philosophy come from (Publication 103). |
| [WINS](https://www.wins.org/) | World Institute for Nuclear Security — security (the second "S") professional development. |

## Professional societies (join one!)

| Society | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [American Nuclear Society (ANS)](https://www.ans.org/) | The largest — student membership is cheap, local/young-member sections are active, and [ANS Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news) is worth following. |
| [European Nuclear Society (ENS)](https://www.euronuclear.org/) | Europe's umbrella society with national member societies and a Young Generation Network. |
| [International Youth Nuclear Congress (IYNC)](https://iync.org/) | The global young-generation network — conferences, mentoring, and a friendly door into the industry. |
| National societies | Most countries have one (e.g., SFEN France, KTfK Germany, SNI Spain, AESJ Japan, and more). Search yours — student rates are the industry's best deal. |

## Regulators (free knowledge mines)

| Regulator | Why to know it |
| --- | --- |
| [US NRC](https://www.nrc.gov/) | The most transparent regulator: glossary, training videos, ADAMS archive, NUREG reports. Even non-Americans learn from it. |
| [STUK](https://www.stuk.fi/en) (Finland) | Small, respected, pragmatic — and the regulator of the first deep geological repository program. |
| [ASN](https://www.asn.fr/) (France), [ONR](https://www.onr.org.uk/) (UK) | Other open, well-documented regulators for comparison. |
| [WENRA](https://www.wenra.org/) | The association of European regulators; publishes reference levels. |

## Education & research institutions to know

- University programs: MIT NSE, UC Berkeley NE, Michigan NERS, Penn State, Texas A&M, Cambridge, Imperial, Delft, Chalmers, KTH, Imperial — most publish course materials and research openly.
- National labs: [ORNL](https://www.ornl.gov/), [ANL](https://www.anl.gov/), [INL](https://www.inl.gov/), [PNL/PNNL](https://www.pnnl.gov/), [CEA](https://www.cea.fr/) (France), [JAEA](https://www.jaea.go.jp/english/) (Japan) — sources of tools, data, and reports.
- IAEA [Nuclear Energy Management & training programs](https://www.iaea.org/services) — professional courses.
