## Lecture notes & course archives

- [MIT OCW — Nuclear Science & Engineering department](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Nuclear%20Engineering) — filter the full course list (the search link covers it)
- University course pages — many nuclear engineering departments (MIT, Berkeley, Michigan, Penn State, Cambridge, Delft, Imperial, Chalmers…) publish lecture notes openly; search `<topic> lecture notes site:.edu`

## Study strategy

1. Pick **one** primary course (22.01 if starting from zero) — don't hoard.
2. Follow the [nuclear-101](https://github.com/nuclear-community/nuclear-101) module sequence; dip into courses at the matching week.
3. Do the problem sets. Watching lectures without solving problems is entertainment, not study.
