# 📚 Textbooks

The canonical shelf — what nuclear engineers actually read in university and keep on their desks. Paid entries are marked; everything at the top is free.

## Free & excellent

| Resource | Level | Access | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *DOE Fundamentals Handbook — Nuclear Physics and Reactor Theory* (DOE-HDBK-1019, Vol. 1 & 2) | 🟢 | free | Written to train US reactor operators: concise, correct, no prerequisites beyond algebra. The best free starting point in existence. Search the report number. |
| *DOE Fundamentals Handbook — Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer, and Fluid Flow* (DOE-HDBK-1012, 3 vols.) | 🟢 | free | Same series, for the thermal side. |
| *DOE Fundamentals Handbook — Instrumentation and Control* (DOE-HDBK-1013) | 🟢 | free | Detector and plant instrumentation basics. |
| IAEA, *Radiation, People and the Environment* | 🟢 | free | Short, beautifully clear booklet on radiation in everyday life. |
| IAEA *Nuclear Energy Series* reports | 🟡 | free | Dozens of technical reports on the fuel cycle, reactors, waste — the international reference library. |
| IAEA *Safety Standards* (SSR/GSR series) | 🔴 | free | The global basis of nuclear regulation. SSR-2/1 for reactor safety. |

## The canonical shelf (paid)

### Introductory nuclear engineering

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lamarsh & Baratta, *Introduction to Nuclear Engineering* (4th ed.) | 🟢→🟡 | THE standard first textbook: physics → reactors → radiation, in one arc. |
| Lewis, *Fundamentals of Nuclear Reactor Physics* | 🟢→🟡 | Slimmer and gentler alternative to Lamarsh, with clean math. |
| Shultis & Faw (w/ Dunn), *Fundamentals of Nuclear Science and Engineering* | 🟢→🟡 | Broad and readable; pairs well with lectures. |

### Nuclear physics

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Krane, *Introductory Nuclear Physics* | 🟡 | The classic physics-side text: decay, reactions, models. |
| Wong, *Introductory Nuclear Physics* | 🟡 | The rigorous alternative to Krane. |

### Reactor physics & analysis

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duderstadt & Hamilton, *Nuclear Reactor Analysis* | 🔴 | The graduate standard — diffusion, transport, kinetics, everything. |
| Stacey, *Nuclear Reactor Physics* (2nd/3rd ed.) | 🟡→🔴 | Modern, comprehensive, slightly friendlier than D&H. |
| Henry, *Nuclear-Reactor Analysis* | 🔴 | Classic MIT treatment; the diffusion-theory chapters are legendary. |

### Thermal-hydraulics & plant systems

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Todreas & Kazimi, *Nuclear Systems I & II* (2nd ed.) | 🟡→🔴 | The thermal-hydraulics bible. |
| El-Wakil, *Nuclear Heat Transport* | 🟡 | Compact, physical, readable. |
| Glasstone & Sesonske, *Nuclear Reactor Engineering* (4th ed.) | 🟡 | Whole-plant view: safety systems, containment, operations. |
| Knief, *Nuclear Engineering* (2nd ed.) | 🟡 | Commercial power technology end-to-end. |

### Radiation detection & protection

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knoll, *Radiation Detection and Measurement* (4th ed.) | 🟡→🔴 | The detection bible — every lab owns one. |
| Turner, *Atoms, Radiation, and Radiation Protection* (3rd ed.) | 🟢→🟡 | The gentlest correct treatment of interaction physics and protection. |
| Martin, *Physics for Radiation Protection* (3rd ed.) | 🟡 | Deep practical protection physics. |
| Shultis & Faw, *Radiation Shielding* | 🔴 | The shielding reference. |

### Fuel cycle & advanced topics

| Book | Level | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crossland (ed.), *Nuclear Fuel Cycle Science and Engineering* | 🟡→🔴 | Front end to back end, modern. |
| Glasstone & Dolan, *The Effects of Nuclear Weapons* | 🔴 | Not engineering — but the physics reference everyone cites for prompt effects. Read critically. |

> **Buying advice:** university libraries carry all of these; international editions and older editions (Lamarsh 3rd, Knoll 3rd) are usually 90% as good for self-study at a fraction of the price.
